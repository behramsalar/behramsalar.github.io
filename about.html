Brain networks, cognition and neurodegeneration.

I am a physician and neuroscientist. My research asks how brain networks give rise to cognition and behavior, using functional and structural neuroimaging (fMRI, DTI, EEG) together with computational and machine learning methods. I focus on how network changes relate to cognitive and affective symptoms in neurodegenerative and neuropsychiatric disorders, mainly Parkinson’s and Alzheimer’s disease: apathy, visual hallucinations, and cognitive decline.

I am an Assistant Professor of Physiology at the International School of Medicine, Istanbul Medipol University. I teach physiology, neuroimaging and programming for neuroscience, and supervise MSc and PhD projects. Before research I worked as an emergency physician.
